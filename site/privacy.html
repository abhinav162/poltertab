Privacy policy

Draft shell. This is placeholder text matching what is true today. The final wording lands with the launch copy pack and will replace this section.

PolterTab is a local tool. It runs entirely on your own computer and drives the Chrome browser you already use.

What we collect

Nothing. PolterTab has no servers of its own, no account, and no analytics or telemetry. We do not receive your browsing data, your credentials, or anything the agent does in your browser.

Where your data lives

On your machine. The MCP server and the Chrome extension communicate over a local connection. Anything the agent reads or does stays between your browser, the local server, and the AI client you connected — none of it passes through PolterTab.

Files and uploads

The agent cannot read or upload files from your computer unless you explicitly allowlist the folders they can come from. Uploads are refused by default.

Permissions

The extension requests the Chrome permissions it needs to act in your browser on your behalf. Because it drives your real, signed-in profile, use it only on sites and accounts you are authorized to access.

Open source

The code is public on GitHub and the package is published to npm with provenance, so you can verify exactly what runs.
